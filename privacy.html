Privacy policy

Effective 7 November 2026

In short

Thlick does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

This policy covers the Thlick app for macOS, both the Mac App Store version and the download from this website, and this website.

What you type

Thlick needs the Input Monitoring permission to notice key presses in other apps. Its key listener is listen-only, so it cannot change, block or add key presses.

For each key press, Thlick works out which kind of key it was (a letter or number, space, return, delete or a modifier key) and where that key sits on the keyboard drawn on screen. It uses that to play a sound and light up the key. It does not work out which character you typed, and nothing about your key presses is saved, logged or sent anywhere.

When you type in a password field, macOS turns on secure input and Thlick ignores your key presses completely.

Microphone

To go quiet during calls, Thlick asks macOS whether another app is using the microphone. Thlick never uses the microphone itself, never hears or records any audio, and does not ask for microphone permission.

Other apps

To honour your per-app exclusions, Thlick checks which app is in front. When you add an app to the list, Thlick keeps only that app's name and identifier.

Your settings

Your settings, shortcuts and excluded apps are stored only on your Mac, in Thlick's own folder at ~/Library/Containers/com.surajkhamkar.thlick. To remove them, delete the app and that folder. Nothing is stored anywhere else, so there is nothing for us to delete.

This website

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its font from Google Fonts. As part of serving them, GitHub and Google receive your IP address, under their own privacy policies.
  • To find the newest version, the download button reads a file on this site, or asks GitHub if that fails.
  • Your phosphor colour and scanline choices are saved in your browser and never leave it. The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Thlick is made by Suraj Khamkar. For questions about privacy, open an issue at github.com/khamkarsuraj/thlick/issues.
